FBR / TAX SERVICES - COMPLETE CITIZEN GUIDE
===========================================
Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) | Iris Portal
Applicable: All Pakistani Citizens & Businesses | Updated: 2025

SECTION 1: WHAT IS NTN?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NTN (National Tax Number) is your unique tax identity number issued
by FBR. It is required for:
- Filing income tax returns
- Opening business bank accounts
- Getting government contracts
- Importing/exporting goods
- Property purchase above Rs. 5 million
- Vehicle registration (commercial)
- Becoming an Active Taxpayer (Filer)

For individual salaried persons: Your CNIC IS your NTN.
For businesses / companies: A separate NTN is issued.

SECTION 2: NTN REGISTRATION (Individual / Salaried)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ONLINE (Recommended):
1. Visit: iris.fbr.gov.pk
2. Click "Registration for Unregistered Person"
3. Enter your CNIC and mobile number
4. OTP will be sent to your mobile
5. Fill in your personal and income details
6. Submit — NTN is usually your CNIC number confirmed

DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
- CNIC (original)
- Mobile number registered on CNIC
- Email address
- Bank account number (IBAN)
- Employer details (for salaried persons)

IN-PERSON (RTO Office):
- Visit nearest Regional Tax Office (RTO)
- Fill NTN registration form
- Submit with CNIC copy and employment letter

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 3: HOW TO CHECK FILER STATUS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FILER = Person who has filed their income tax return this year.
FILER STATUS gives you lower withholding tax rates on:
- Bank transactions
- Property purchase/sale
- Vehicle purchase
- Prize bonds

HOW TO CHECK:
Method 1 — Website:
  fbr.gov.pk → "Verify Taxpayer" → Enter CNIC

Method 2 — SMS:
  Type: ATL (space) CNIC-number
  Send to: 9966
  Example: ATL 3740512345678

Method 3 — IRIS Portal:
  iris.fbr.gov.pk → Login → Dashboard shows your status

SECTION 4: HOW TO FILE INCOME TAX RETURN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHO MUST FILE:
- Annual income above Rs. 6,00,000 (6 Lakh)
- Any person with NTN
- Business owners / self-employed
- Property owners
- Freelancers earning abroad

DEADLINE: Usually September 30 each year (extended sometimes)

STEP-BY-STEP ONLINE FILING:
1. Login to: iris.fbr.gov.pk
2. Go to "Declaration" → "Income Tax Return"
3. Select the tax year (July–June)
4. Fill in:
   - Salary/income details
   - Bank accounts
   - Property owned
   - Investments
   - Business income (if any)
5. Review and submit
6. Download your filed return acknowledgment (keep this)

DOCUMENTS TO KEEP READY:
- Salary slips / employer certificate (Form-16)
- Bank statements
- Rental income records
- Any investment certificates
- Utility bills for assets declared

SECTION 5: BUSINESS / COMPANY NTN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

DOCUMENTS FOR BUSINESS NTN:
- CNIC of owner / all partners / directors
- Business registration certificate (SECP or Partnership deed)
- Utility bill for business address
- Bank account details of business
- Letterhead of company

PROCESS:
1. Visit iris.fbr.gov.pk → "Registration" → "Business"
2. Upload all documents
3. NTN issued within 3–5 working days

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 6: SALES TAX (STRN) REGISTRATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

STRN (Sales Tax Registration Number) required if:
- Annual business turnover exceeds Rs. 10 million
- You supply taxable goods/services
- You import goods

Register via: iris.fbr.gov.pk → "Sales Tax Registration"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 7: FREELANCERS / IT EXPORTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Good news for freelancers: IT export income is EXEMPT from tax
under current policy (verify current year's Finance Act).

TO DECLARE FREELANCE INCOME:
1. Register NTN on IRIS
2. File annual return showing foreign remittance income
3. Attach bank statement showing inward remittances
4. Claim exemption under IT export category

SECTION 8: PENALTIES FOR NOT FILING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- Late filing penalty: Rs. 1,000 per day (minimum Rs. 10,000)
- Non-filer: Higher withholding tax on banking, property, vehicles
- Non-filer property purchase: 4% WHT (vs 2% for filers)
- Non-filer vehicle: Higher registration tax
