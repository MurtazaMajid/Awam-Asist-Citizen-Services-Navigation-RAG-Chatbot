BISP AND EHSAAS PROGRAMS - CITIZEN GUIDE
Pakistan Social Safety Net Services

===========================================
WHAT IS BISP?
===========================================

The Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP) is Pakistan's largest federal cash transfer program for low-income families. It provides financial assistance to poor and deserving households across all provinces.

The program is managed through the 8171 helpline and web portal.
Official Website: bisp.gov.pk
SMS Helpline: Send CNIC number to 8171
Phone Helpline: 0800-26477 (toll free)

===========================================
BENAZIR KAFAALAT PROGRAM
===========================================

What is it?
Monthly financial support for low-income women. It is the main cash transfer program under BISP.

Payment Amount (2026):
- Regular beneficiaries: Rs. 14,500 per quarter (every 3 months)
- Payments are distributed via banks, ATMs, and BISP Tehsil Offices
- HBL Konnect and Bank Alfalah outlets are also used

Who is eligible?
- Women who are Pakistani nationals with a valid CNIC
- Household monthly income below the poverty line threshold (PMT score below 32)
- Not a government employee (federal or provincial)
- Spouse is not a government employee
- Does not own more than 3 acres of farmland or more than 80 square yards of residential land
- Not already enrolled in another government support program
- Family registered in the NSER (National Socio-Economic Registry) survey database

Who is NOT eligible?
- Government employees or their spouses
- People receiving a pension from the government
- Families owning large amounts of land or property
- People who have traveled abroad recently (executive travel)
- Families whose members applied for CNIC through executive fee option (3 or more members)

How to check eligibility:
Method 1 - SMS: Send your 13-digit CNIC number to 8171. You will receive a reply in Urdu or English showing your eligibility status.
Method 2 - Online: Visit 8171.bisp.gov.pk, enter your CNIC number, fill in the captcha, and click Submit.
Method 3 - Visit: Go to your nearest BISP Tehsil Office with your original CNIC.

How to register:
1. Visit your nearest BISP office or NSER Survey Center
2. Bring your original CNIC
3. Provide family details and income information
4. Complete biometric verification
5. After verification, you will receive confirmation of eligibility
6. Track your registration online through the 8171 portal

PMT Score:
BISP uses a Poverty Means Test (PMT) score to determine eligibility. The lower your PMT score, the poorer you are considered. Currently the threshold is PMT score below 32 to be eligible.

How to collect payment:
- Visit any HBL ATM with your Smart Card
- Visit authorized BISP retailers or Citizen Shops
- Visit BISP Tehsil Office with your original CNIC and complete biometric verification
- New beneficiaries must first visit a BISP-authorized retailer for initial verification

Important: BISP registration is valid for 3 years. After 3 years, you must complete the dynamic survey again to remain eligible.

===========================================
BENAZIR TALEEMI WAZAIF (EDUCATION STIPENDS)
===========================================

What is it?
Education stipends for children of BISP Kafaalat beneficiaries. It encourages school enrollment and attendance.

Who is eligible?
Children of registered BISP Kafaalat beneficiaries who are enrolled in school.

Stipend Amounts (2026):
- Primary level: Rs. 2,500 per quarter
- Secondary/Higher level: Rs. 4,500 per quarter
- Payments started from April 6, 2026

Important: If the mother's Kafaalat status becomes inactive, the child's stipend is also stopped. Keep the mother's registration active.

===========================================
BENAZIR NASHONUMA PROGRAMME
===========================================

What is it?
Conditional cash transfer for pregnant women and mothers with children under 2 years old. Focuses on nutrition and health.

Target: Reduce stunting in children under 2 years.

===========================================
HOW TO UPDATE YOUR INFORMATION
===========================================

If your status shows registered but you are not receiving payments:
1. Visit your nearest BISP office
2. Complete the dynamic survey update
3. Provide updated CNIC and family details
4. Wait for re-verification (can take a few weeks)

If you were removed from the program:
Beneficiaries who did not update their information after 3 years may be removed. Visit BISP office and complete the dynamic survey to be re-enrolled.

===========================================
FRAUD PREVENTION - IMPORTANT WARNINGS
===========================================

BISP services are 100% FREE. Never pay anyone claiming to help with registration or payment.

Do not:
- Pay any middleman for BISP registration
- Share your Smart Card PIN with anyone
- Trust anyone claiming to guarantee BISP enrollment for a fee
- Use unofficial websites or apps for BISP checks

Only use:
- Official SMS to 8171
- Official website: 8171.bisp.gov.pk or bisp.gov.pk
- Official BISP offices
- Authorized HBL ATMs and partner banks

If you suspect fraud, report immediately to BISP helpline: 0800-26477
