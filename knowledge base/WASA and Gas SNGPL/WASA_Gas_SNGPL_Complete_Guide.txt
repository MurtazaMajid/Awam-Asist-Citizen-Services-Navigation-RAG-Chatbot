============================================
WASA (Water & Sanitation Authority) | SNGPL | SSGC

PART A: WATER & SEWERAGE (WASA)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SECTION A1: NEW WATER CONNECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ELIGIBILITY:
- Property owner in WASA jurisdiction
- Property must be completed (grey structure minimum)
- No existing illegal water connection

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Application form (from WASA office)
- CNIC copy (owner)
- Proof of property ownership (Fard / Registry copy)
- Site map / layout plan of property
- Latest paid utility bill of same address (if any)

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. Visit nearest WASA office in your city
2. Collect and fill application form (or download online)
3. Submit application with all documents + fee
4. WASA inspector visits site to verify
5. Connection approved and pipe laid
6. Meter installed

CONNECTION FEE:
- Varies by connection size (¾ inch, 1 inch, etc.)
- Residential: Rs. 8,000–25,000 (approximate, check locally)
- Always confirm current fee at WASA office

WASA bill includes:
- Water charges (per unit consumed)
- Sewerage charges
- Meter rent

HOW TO PAY WASA BILL:
- At any bank branch (bring bill)
- Easypaisa / JazzCash (enter Reference Number)
- WASA office cash counter
- Online (check your city's WASA website)

CHECK BILL ONLINE:
- Lahore WASA:      wasa.punjab.gov.pk
- Rawalpindi WASA:  rwasa.gop.pk
- Faisalabad WASA:  fwasa.faisalabad.gov.pk

SECTION A3: WASA COMPLAINTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

COMMON COMPLAINTS:
- No water supply / low pressure
- Burst pipe (emergency)
- Wrong bill amount
- Meter not working
- Sewerage blockage / overflow

HOW TO FILE COMPLAINT:
Method 1 — Call WASA helpline (see Section A4)
Method 2 — Visit nearest WASA sub-office / zone office
Method 3 — Pakistan Citizens Portal app (for slow response)
Method 4 — Online complaint portal (where available)

EMERGENCY (burst pipe / sewerage overflow):
Call emergency number immediately — teams dispatched same day.

PART B: GAS SERVICES (SNGPL)

Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, AJK, Gilgit-Baltistan.
For Sindh & Balochistan: SSGC (Sui Southern Gas Company)

SECTION B1: NEW GAS CONNECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- CNIC (original + copy)
- Proof of ownership (Fard, Registry, or allotment letter)
- Recent electricity bill for the same property
- No-Objection Certificate from landlord (if renting)
- Completed SNGPL application form

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. Visit SNGPL regional office OR apply online:
   sngas.com → "New Connection"
2. Fill application form and submit with documents
3. Pay application fee (Rs. 2,000–5,000 approx.)
4. SNGPL survey team visits your property
5. Technical feasibility confirmed
6. Demand Notice issued (you pay remaining charges)
7. Gas meter installed and connection activated

IMPORTANT: Connection is given based on availability of gas
pressure in your area. Some areas have moratorium (no new
connections) — check with SNGPL first.

SECTION B2: ONLINE APPLICATION (SNGPL)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Go to: sngas.com
2. Click "New Connection" → "Domestic"
3. Fill online form with CNIC and property details
4. Upload scanned documents
5. Get application reference number
6. Track application status on same portal

SECTION B3: SNGPL BILL — PAYMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CHECK BILL ONLINE:
- sngas.com → "Customer Services" → "Bill Inquiry"
- Enter your Consumer Number (on your bill)

PAY BILL:
- Bank branch (all major banks)
- Easypaisa / JazzCash / UPaisa
- Online banking (internet banking)
- SNGPL cash counters

WHAT IS THE CONSUMER NUMBER?
It's a unique 10-digit number printed on top of your gas bill.
Keep it safe — needed for all services.

SECTION B4: SNGPL COMPLAINTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

COMMON ISSUES:
- Gas supply stopped / low pressure
- Gas leak (EMERGENCY — call immediately!)
- Wrong bill
- Meter not working
- New connection delay

GAS LEAK — EMERGENCY PROCEDURE:
1. Do NOT switch on/off any electrical switches
2. Do NOT light any flame
3. Open all windows and doors
4. Leave the premises immediately
5. Call SNGPL emergency: 1199 (24/7)
6. Do NOT re-enter until cleared by SNGPL team

FILE REGULAR COMPLAINT:
- Call: 0800-00992 (toll free)
- Online: sngas.com → "Complaint"
- Visit nearest SNGPL office
- Pakistan Citizens Portal app

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

If gas was disconnected due to unpaid bills:
1. Pay all outstanding dues (with surcharge)
2. Visit SNGPL office with payment receipt + CNIC
3. Request reconnection
4. SNGPL team visits within 3–7 working days
