SEHAT SAHULAT CARD - COMPLETE CITIZEN GUIDE
===========================================
Punjab Health Initiative Management Company (PHIMC)
Applicable: Punjab Residents | Updated: 2025

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 1: WHAT IS SEHAT SAHULAT CARD?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Sehat Sahulat Card is Punjab government's free health insurance scheme.
It provides FREE inpatient (admitted) treatment up to Rs. 10 Lakh per
family per year at empanelled government AND private hospitals.

WHO IS ELIGIBLE:
- All registered families in Punjab (under BISP / Ehsaas database)
- Later expanded to cover ALL Punjab families (universal coverage)
- No income limit — every Punjab family is included
- Family means: husband, wife, and up to 4 dependent children

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 2: WHAT IS COVERED?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

COVERED TREATMENTS:
✔ Major surgical operations
✔ Heart surgeries (bypass, angioplasty)
✔ Cancer treatment (chemotherapy, radiotherapy)
✔ Kidney dialysis
✔ Maternity (normal delivery + C-section)
✔ Burns treatment
✔ Accident/trauma emergency care
✔ ICU and ventilator care
✔ Neurosurgery
✔ Eye surgery (cataracts, retina)
✔ Orthopedic surgery (fractures, joint replacement)

NOT COVERED (Outpatient / OPD):
✘ Routine doctor visits (OPD)
✘ Medicines for home use
✘ Dental treatment (non-emergency)
✘ Cosmetic surgery
✘ Pre-existing conditions that are stable (some exclusions apply)

COVERAGE LIMIT:
- Rs. 10,00,000 (10 Lakh) per family per year
- Burns cases: up to Rs. 15,00,000

SECTION 3: HOW TO CHECK IF YOU ARE ENROLLED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

METHOD 1 — SMS:
Send your CNIC number to: 8500
You will receive an SMS confirming if you are enrolled.

METHOD 2 — Website:
Visit: sehat.punjab.gov.pk
Enter your CNIC to check enrollment status.

METHOD 3 — At Hospital:
Visit any empanelled hospital's Sehat Desk.
Give your CNIC — they will check on the spot.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 4: HOW TO USE THE CARD AT HOSPITAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. Go to any empanelled (approved) hospital
2. Find the "Sehat Sahulat Desk" or "Help Desk" at reception
3. Show your CNIC (the card itself is your CNIC — no separate card needed)
4. Hospital verifies your enrollment via the system
5. Treatment is pre-authorized — you pay NOTHING for covered treatment
6. Hospital directly claims from government

IMPORTANT:
- Always go to an EMPANELLED hospital — not all hospitals are included
- For emergencies: go to nearest empanelled hospital
- Keep your CNIC with you at all times during treatment

SECTION 5: HOW TO ENROLL (IF NOT ALREADY)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Most Punjab families are auto-enrolled via NADRA database.
If you are NOT enrolled:

STEP 1: Visit nearest BISP/Ehsaas office OR
        Tehsil Municipal Administration (TMA) office

STEP 2: Bring documents:
        - Original CNIC (husband + wife)
        - B-Forms of children
        - Proof of Punjab residence (utility bill / domicile)

STEP 3: Request enrollment in Sehat Sahulat scheme

STEP 4: Verify via SMS (8500) after 7–14 days

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 6: FIND EMPANELLED HOSPITALS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Website: sehat.punjab.gov.pk → "Find Hospital"
Or call helpline to ask for nearest empanelled hospital.

Types of empanelled hospitals:
- Government hospitals (DHQ, THQ, teaching hospitals)
- Approved private hospitals
- Specialized hospitals (cancer, cardiac, children)

SECTION 7: COMPLAINTS & ISSUES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

IF HOSPITAL REFUSES TREATMENT:
1. Ask to speak with the hospital's focal person
2. Call helpline immediately: 0311-7786262
3. File complaint at sehat.punjab.gov.pk

COMMON ISSUES & SOLUTIONS:
- "CNIC not found" → Verify enrollment via SMS 8500 first
- "Limit exhausted" → Check remaining balance via helpline
- "Procedure not covered" → Ask for alternative covered procedure code
- "Hospital not empanelled" → Find nearest empanelled hospital first
