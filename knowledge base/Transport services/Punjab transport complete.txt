Punjab Public Transport Services - Complete Citizen Guide
==========================================================

OVERVIEW OF PUBLIC TRANSPORT IN PUNJAB
---------------------------------------
Punjab government operates multiple public transport services across major cities including Lahore, Rawalpindi, Islamabad, and Multan. These include Metro Bus, Orange Line Train, Speedo Bus, and Electro Bus services.

==========================================================
T-CASH CARD (CM Punjab T-Cash Card)
==========================================================
The T-Cash Card is a rechargeable smart banking card for cashless travel on all Punjab public transport services. It was introduced by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz as part of Digital Punjab vision.

Works on:
- Metro Bus (Lahore, Rawalpindi-Islamabad, Multan)
- Orange Line Metro Train (Lahore)
- Speedo Buses
- Electro Buses
- Other integrated transit services across Punjab

Benefits:
- One card for all transport services — no need for separate tickets
- Works as a debit card for everyday shopping and transactions
- Saves up to Rs. 2,000-3,000 per month on fares
- PIN protected and secure
- Lost card can be blocked via helpline
- Students get free or subsidized fares on select routes
- Special provisions for women and disabled persons

Eligibility:
- Any Punjab resident using public transport
- Must be 18 years or above with valid CNIC
- Minors can apply via guardian with B-Form
- Verified through NADRA
- Students from grade 6 to university eligible for student card with subsidized fares

Card details:
- Issuance fee: Rs. 130
- Card validity: 5 years (renewable)
- Minimum recharge: Rs. 100
- Over 500,000 cards issued as of October 2025

How to apply:
Option 1 - Online:
1. Visit etransit.punjab.gov.pk
2. Click Apply Online
3. Enter CNIC details (verified by NADRA)
4. Enter mobile number and OTP verification
5. Pay Rs. 130 issuance fee
6. Submit and wait for confirmation SMS
7. Collect card from nearest BOP branch or Metro station
   OR use home delivery service

Option 2 - Mobile App:
- Download e-Transit Punjab app
- Follow same registration steps

Option 3 - In person:
- Visit nearest Metro or Orange Line station
- Visit designated Bank of Punjab (BOP) booths at transport stations

How to recharge your T-Cash Card:
- Visit any Bank of Punjab (BOP) branch or ATM
- Use BOP Digital App for online recharge
- Recharge at Metro or Orange Line stations
- Authorized recharge points across Punjab

How to check card status:
- Check online at etransit.punjab.gov.pk
- Use the e-Transit mobile app
- Call helpline: 1762

Lost card:
- Block lost card immediately by calling helpline 1762
- Apply for replacement through portal or app

METRO BUS SERVICE
==========================================================
Metro Bus is a rapid bus transit service operating in:
- Lahore (multiple routes)
- Rawalpindi-Islamabad corridor
- Multan

Features:
- Air-conditioned buses
- Dedicated bus lanes
- Fixed stops and schedules
- Cashless payment via T-Cash Card
- Separate seating for women

==========================================================
ORANGE LINE METRO TRAIN
==========================================================
Orange Line is Lahore's urban metro train service.

Features:
- Fully air-conditioned
- Covers major areas of Lahore
- Cashless payment via T-Cash Card
- Fast and frequent service

==========================================================
SPEEDO BUS SERVICE
==========================================================
Speedo Bus is an air-conditioned bus service operating in Lahore and other Punjab cities.

Features:
- Air-conditioned
- Fixed routes and stops
- T-Cash Card accepted

PUNJAB TRANSPORT COMPANY (PTC)
==========================================================
PTC manages intercity and intracity transport services across Punjab.

PTC Head Office:
1st Floor, EOBI Building
Plot #66, Shahrah Nazria Pakistan
Near Ayub Chowk, Johar Town, Lahore

Challan Offices:
- 134 Green Town Old PRTC Depot, Industrial Area, Kot Lakhpat, Lahore
- Railway Station, Lahore

Commuter Support Center:
- Dedicated support for complaints about routes, fares, lost items
- If you lose something on a PTC bus, contact the commuter support center
- PTC has recovered and returned lost items including cash, mobile phones, wallets
