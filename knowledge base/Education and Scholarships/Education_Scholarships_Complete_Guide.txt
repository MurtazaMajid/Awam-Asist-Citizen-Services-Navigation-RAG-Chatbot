SECTION 1: HEC NEED-BASED SCHOLARSHIP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHAT IT IS:
Higher Education Commission (HEC) gives free scholarships to
financially deserving students enrolled in universities.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Enrolled in HEC-recognized university (public or private)
- Family income below Rs. 45,000/month
- At least 60% marks in last exam
- Pakistani citizen with valid CNIC/B-Form

COVERS:
- Full tuition fee
- Monthly stipend (Rs. 3,000–6,000)
- Some hostels included

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Visit: hec.gov.pk → "Scholarships" → "Need-Based"
2. Register with CNIC and university enrollment details
3. Fill application form online
4. Upload: income certificate, domicile, marks sheet, CNIC
5. Submit before deadline (usually announced twice a year)

DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
- CNIC or B-Form
- University enrollment letter
- Last exam marks sheet
- Family income certificate (from Union Council or employer)
- Domicile certificate
- Bank account (for stipend)

HELPLINE: 051-111-119-432
Website:  hec.gov.pk/scholarships

SECTION 2: PM LAPTOP SCHEME
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHAT IT IS:
Prime Minister's scheme to provide free laptops to top-performing
students in universities and colleges.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Enrolled in HEC-recognized institution
- CGPA of 3.0 or above (or equivalent percentage)
- Pakistani citizen
- Not currently receiving another government laptop

PROCESS:
1. Universities nominate eligible students based on CGPA
2. HEC verifies and approves
3. Laptops distributed via university
4. No direct application — done through your institution

ACTION: Contact your university's HEC focal person / student affairs
        department to ask if you are nominated.

SECTION 3: HEC STUDENT LOAN (INTEREST-FREE)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHAT IT IS:
HEC provides interest-free education loans for students who cannot
afford university fees. Repaid after graduation and employment.

ELIGIBILITY:
- Enrolled in HEC-recognized university
- Family income below threshold
- Good academic standing

REPAYMENT:
- Repayment starts 1 year after graduation
- No interest charged
- Flexible installments

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Visit: hec.gov.pk → "Loan Programs"
2. Apply online with required documents
3. University verifies enrollment
4. Loan disbursed to university account

SECTION 4: EHSAAS UNDERGRADUATE SCHOLARSHIP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHAT IT IS:
Federal government scholarship for students from low-income families
to attend universities — covers full fees + stipend.

ELIGIBILITY:
- BISP/Ehsaas registered family OR income below Rs. 45,000/month
- Admission in HEC-recognized university
- Minimum 60% marks in Intermediate / FSc / FA

AMOUNT:
- Full tuition fee paid directly to university
- Monthly stipend: Rs. 4,000
- Hostel fee where applicable

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Check: ehsaas.nadra.gov.pk or hec.gov.pk
2. Apply online when admissions cycle opens
3. Upload: CNIC, income certificate, admission letter, marks

SECTION 5: PUNJAB SCHOOL ENROLLMENT (CHILD ADMISSION)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

AGE FOR SCHOOL:
- Grade 1 (Pakki): 5–6 years old
- Katchi (KG): 4–5 years old

GOVERNMENT SCHOOL ADMISSION DOCUMENTS:
- Child's B-Form (NADRA)
- Parent's CNIC
- Recent passport-size photograph
- Transfer certificate (if transferring from another school)

PROCESS:
1. Visit nearest government school in your area
2. Meet the Head Teacher
3. Submit documents
4. Admission is FREE in all government schools
5. Free textbooks provided by Punjab Curriculum & Textbook Board

FREE ITEMS IN GOVERNMENT SCHOOL:
✔ Textbooks (free)
✔ Uniform stipend (in some schemes)
✔ Free meals (some schools — Parha Likha Punjab)

DUPLICATE CERTIFICATE (if lost):

- Available online on BISE websites
- Search by roll number
