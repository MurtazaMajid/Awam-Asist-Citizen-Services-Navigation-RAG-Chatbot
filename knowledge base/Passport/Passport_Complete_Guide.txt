PASSPORT SERVICES - COMPLETE CITIZEN GUIDE
==========================================
Directorate General of Immigration & Passports (DGIP)
Applicable: All Pakistani Citizens | Updated: 2025

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 1: TYPES OF PASSPORT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Machine Readable Passport (MRP) — Standard
2. e-Passport (Biometric chip) — Now available across Pakistan
3. Official Passport — For government employees on duty
4. Diplomatic Passport — For diplomats

NOTE: e-Passport is now the recommended option — more secure and
accepted worldwide.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 2: NEW PASSPORT (First Time)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
- Original CNIC (18 years and above)
- B-Form / Child Registration Certificate (under 18)
- Original CNIC of parent (for minors)
- 2 recent passport-size photographs (white background)
- Fee payment receipt (bank challan or online payment)

STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS:
1. Visit: passport.gov.pk to fill online form OR
   go directly to your Regional Passport Office (RPO)
2. Fill passport application form (Form-1)
3. Pay the fee at HBL / UBL / MCB or online
4. Visit RPO with all documents for biometric (fingerprints + photo)
5. Collect passport after processing time

PROCESSING TIME:
- Normal:     30 days
- Urgent:     7–10 working days (higher fee)
- Fast Track: 2–3 working days (available in major cities)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 3: PASSPORT RENEWAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
- Original old/expired passport
- Original CNIC (updated)
- 2 passport-size photographs (white background)
- Fee payment receipt

ONLINE RENEWAL (Inland):
- Available at: passport.gov.pk/online-renewal
- Upload scanned documents
- Pay fee online
- Visit RPO only for biometric — no long queues

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. Log on to passport.gov.pk
2. Select "Online Renewal"
3. Fill in your details and upload documents
4. Pay fee online (debit/credit card or bank)
5. Get appointment for biometric at nearest RPO
6. Collect passport by courier or from office

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 4: FEE STRUCTURE (2024–2025)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NORMAL (30 days):
- 36 pages: Rs. 3,000
- 72 pages: Rs. 5,000

URGENT (7–10 days):
- 36 pages: Rs. 5,000
- 72 pages: Rs. 8,000

FAST TRACK (2–3 days):
- 36 pages: Rs. 9,000
- 72 pages: Rs. 12,000

NOTE: Fees are subject to change. Always confirm at passport.gov.pk

WHERE TO PAY:
- HBL, UBL, MCB, ABL branches (bank challan)
- Online via passport.gov.pk (debit/credit card)

SECTION 5: TRACK YOUR PASSPORT STATUS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ONLINE: passport.gov.pk → "Track Application"
Enter your CNIC or application number to check status.

SMS: Send CNIC number to 9988 (some regions)

STATUSES EXPLAINED:
- "Received"       → Application logged in system
- "Under Process"  → Being verified and printed
- "Dispatched"     → Sent to your address / RPO
- "Ready to Collect" → Visit office with original CNIC

SECTION 6: LOST OR STOLEN PASSPORT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

STEPS TO FOLLOW:
1. File FIR at nearest police station (mandatory)
2. Get FIR copy
3. Visit RPO with: FIR copy + CNIC + photographs + fee
4. Fill "Lost Passport" application form
5. A new passport will be issued after verification

ABROAD (Pakistani Embassy/Consulate):
- Report to nearest Pakistani Embassy immediately
- Emergency Travel Document (ETD) can be issued for urgent return

SECTION 8: CHILD PASSPORT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Child's B-Form (original)
- Original CNIC of both parents
- Child's birth certificate
- 2 photographs of child
- Fee payment receipt
- Parent/guardian must be physically present

NOTE: Children under 18 cannot sign their own passport application —
parent/guardian signs on their behalf.

NICOP (National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis):

- Functions as both ID and travel document to Pakistan
- Apply via: id.nadra.gov.pk or nearest Pakistani Embassy
