Zakat & Ushr Department Punjab - Complete Citizen Guide
========================================================

WHAT IS ZAKAT?
--------------
Zakat is a mandatory Islamic charity collected by the Government of Pakistan. The Zakat & Ushr Department of Punjab distributes these funds to deserving (mustahiq) citizens through various programs. Zakat is deducted from PLS (Profit and Loss Sharing) bank accounts on 1st of Ramadan every year.

WHO IS ELIGIBLE FOR ZAKAT?
---------------------------
- Must be a Muslim citizen of Pakistan
- Must be living below the poverty line
- Non-Muslims are not eligible for Zakat

NISAB (MINIMUM THRESHOLD) FOR 2025:
- Nisab for 1445-46 A.H (2025) is Rs. 179,689
- Zakat is deducted from bank accounts holding this amount or more

HOW ZAKAT IS DISTRIBUTED:
- Punjab Zakat & Ushr Council releases funds to District Zakat & Ushr Committees
- District Committees release funds to Local Zakat & Ushr Committees
- Local Committees distribute funds directly to deserving people in their area

========================================================
PROGRAM 1: ZAKAT RASHAN CARD (GUZARA ALLOWANCE)
========================================================
A monthly grant for chronic poor people to safeguard them from hunger.

Amount: PKR 2,000 per month
Payment method: Branchless banking with biometric verification

Eligibility:
- Adult Muslim living below poverty level
- Unemployed
- Preference given to widows and disabled persons
- Not a habitual beggar
- Istehqaq determined by Local Zakat & Ushr Committee of area of permanent residence

How to apply:
- Visit the Chairman of your Local Zakat & Ushr Committee in your area of residence
- The committee will evaluate your financial condition
- If approved, you will receive payment via branchless banking

========================================================
PROGRAM 2: GUZARA ALLOWANCE FOR BLINDS
========================================================
A monthly stipend specifically for blind and visually impaired poor people.

How to apply:
- Apply to Chairman of Local Zakat & Ushr Committee of your area of residence
- Istehqaq is determined by the Local Committee

========================================================
PROGRAM 3: EDUCATION STIPENDS (GENERAL)
========================================================
Financial assistance for poor students to continue their education in government colleges and universities.

Eligible institutions:
- Government colleges, universities, polytechnic institutes
- Professional institutions recognized by the Government of Punjab

Stipend rates per year:
- Intermediate level: Rs. 10,000 per year
- BS/Graduate & Post Graduate: Rs. 15,000 per year
- Engineering/Medical/Computer Science: Rs. 20,000 per year

How to apply:
- Apply to the Head of your educational institution
- Institution forwards application to District Zakat & Ushr Committee
- Istehqaq determined by Local Zakat & Ushr Committee
- Endorsed by scholarship committee of the institution (Head, Class Teacher, District Committee nominee)
- Funds released to institution which pays the student

========================================================
PROGRAM 4: EDUCATION STIPENDS (TECHNICAL)
========================================================
Financial assistance for poor students in vocational and technical training institutes.

Eligible institutions:
- Punjab Vocational Training Council institutes
- Technical and vocational training institutes

How to apply:
- Apply to District Zakat & Ushr Committee through your institution
- Istehqaq determined by Local Zakat & Ushr Committee

========================================================
PROGRAM 5: EDUCATION STIPENDS (DEENI MADARIS)
========================================================
Financial assistance for poor students getting religious education in madrasas.

Eligible institutions:
- Madrasas enlisted with Provincial Zakat Administration

How to apply:
- Apply to Head of your madrasa
- Madrasa forwards to District Zakat & Ushr Committee
- Istehqaq determined by Local Zakat & Ushr Committee of area of residence or where madrasa is located

========================================================
PROGRAM 6: MARRIAGE ASSISTANCE
========================================================
Financial assistance for poor women who cannot afford marriage expenses.

Amount: Rs. 50,000 (one time grant)
Payment method: Branchless banking

Eligibility:
- Any poor, needy and deserving woman
- Or her parent/guardian can apply on her behalf

How to apply:
- Apply to Local Zakat & Ushr Committee
- Committee determines istehqaq and forwards to District Zakat & Ushr Committee
- District Committee releases funds
- Payment made through branchless banking

========================================================
PROGRAM 7: HEALTH CARE
========================================================
Free medical treatment for poor patients at government hospitals.

Covered hospitals include:
- Provincial level and teaching hospitals
- District Headquarter (DHQ) hospitals
- Tehsil Headquarter (THQ) hospitals
- Shoukat Khanum Memorial Hospital Lahore
- Dialysis Center (REDO) Rawalpindi
- Pakistan Thalassemia Welfare Society Rawalpindi
- Ghurki Trust Teaching Hospital Lahore
- Kidney Center Gujrat

What is covered:
- Free medicines
- Medical tests
- Operations
- Artificial limbs
- General ward bed
- Free patient transport
Note: Cash is NOT given to patients. Treatment is provided directly.

Eligibility:
- Istehqaq determined by Local Zakat & Ushr Committee of patient's permanent residence area

How to apply:
- Get Istehqaq certificate from Local Zakat & Ushr Committee of your area
- Submit certificate to Health Welfare Committee of the hospital
- Applications processed by Medical Superintendent

Note: Currently discontinued by Punjab Zakat & Ushr Council for the current financial year.

========================================================
PROGRAM 8: LEPROSY PATIENTS
========================================================
Special assistance for leprosy patients.

Distribution:
- Zakat funds released to District Zakat Committee Rawalpindi
- Distributed through Local Zakat Committee of Ahata Korian to leprosy patients

========================================================
PROGRAM 9: TALIF-E-QALAB
========================================================
One-time financial grant for newly converted Muslims.

Currently available in: Lahore, Rawalpindi and Multan only (pilot project)

Amount: Rs. 150,000 (one time cash grant via crossed cheque)

Eligibility:
- Adult who has converted to Islam
- Must apply within one year of conversion
- Only one person per family can apply

Required documents:
- Muslim conversion certificate from DAR-UL-AFTAA
- CNIC with Muslim name issued by NADRA

How to apply:
- Apply to District Zakat & Ushr Committee of your area
- Committee verifies conversion certificate with DAR-UL-AFTAA
- Antecedents verified by Local Zakat & Ushr Committee Chairman

========================================================
PROGRAM 10: RELEASE OF PRISONERS
========================================================
Financial assistance to help release poor prisoners who cannot pay fines.

How to apply:
- Contact Local Zakat & Ushr Committee for assistance

GENERAL INFORMATION

Contact your Local Zakat & Ushr Committee:
- Every ward, deh (village) and locality has a Local Zakat & Ushr Committee
- They are your first point of contact for all Zakat programs
- Istehqaq (eligibility) must be certified by them for all programs

Website: zakat.punjab.gov.pk
Phone: Contact District Zakat & Ushr Committee of your district
