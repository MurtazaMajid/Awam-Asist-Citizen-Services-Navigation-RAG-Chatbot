NADRA Services - Complete Citizen Guide
========================================

WHAT IS NADRA?
---------------
NADRA (National Database & Registration Authority) is Pakistan's premier government agency responsible for maintaining the national database, issuing identity cards, and providing secure registration services to all citizens.

NADRA Helpline: 051-111-786-100
NADRA Online Portal: id.nadra.gov.pk
Pak ID App: Available on Android and iOS

========================================
SERVICE TIERS - NORMAL, URGENT, EXECUTIVE
========================================
NADRA offers three processing speeds for most services:

Normal: Lowest cost, longest processing time (around 31 days)
Urgent: Higher cost, faster processing (around 12-23 days)
Executive: Highest cost, fastest processing (around 9 days)

========================================
CNIC (COMPUTERIZED NATIONAL IDENTITY CARD)
========================================
The CNIC is the primary identity document for all Pakistani citizens aged 18 and above.

Validity: 10 years
Required for: Voting, banking, SIM registration, government services, travel, property, etc.

CNIC FEES (2025):
New CNIC:
- Normal: Free (31 days)
- Urgent: Rs. 1,150 (12 days)
- Executive: Rs. 2,150 (9 days)

Renewal/Modification/Duplicate:
- Normal: Rs. 400 (31 days)
- Urgent: Rs. 1,150 (12 days)
- Executive: Rs. 2,150 (9 days)

Smart CNIC (SNIC) - latest generation with chip:
- Normal: Rs. 750 (31 days)
- Urgent: Rs. 1,500 (15 days)
- Executive: Rs. 2,500 (9 days)

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR NEW CNIC:
- Original Birth Certificate or B-Form
- CNIC copy of father/mother or guardian
- Proof of residence (utility bill or rental agreement)
- For married women: Marriage certificate

ELIGIBILITY:
- Pakistani citizen aged 18 or above
- Children under 18 must apply for B-Form
- Overseas Pakistanis apply for NICOP

HOW TO APPLY FOR CNIC:
Option 1 - Online:
1. Visit id.nadra.gov.pk or use Pak ID App
2. Fill in your details
3. Pay fee online
4. Visit nearest NADRA Registration Center (NRC) for biometrics

Option 2 - In Person:
1. Visit nearest NADRA Registration Center
2. Bring required documents
3. Fill application form
4. Get photo and fingerprints taken
5. Pay fee at counter
6. Collect CNIC after processing time

LOST CNIC:
- File a police report for lost CNIC
- Bring police report to NADRA center
- Apply for duplicate CNIC
- Fee: Same as renewal

CNIC VERIFICATION:
- Visit NADRA CNIC Verification Portal online
- Enter your CNIC number to check details and status
- Track your application via SMS to 8400

========================================
NICOP (NATIONAL IDENTITY CARD FOR OVERSEAS PAKISTANIS)
========================================
For Pakistani citizens living abroad. Allows dual nationality holders to travel to Pakistan without a visa.

Validity: 10 years

Documents required:
- Pakistani passport (valid or expired)
- Previous CNIC or NICOP
- Parents CNIC/NICOP
- Siblings CNIC/NICOP
- Spouse CNIC (if married)
- Marriage/Divorce certificate (if applicable)
- Union Council birth certificate or B-Form

========================================
B-FORM / CHILD REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE (CRC)
========================================
First identity document for children under 18. Links child to parents in national database.

Validity: Expires when child turns 18 (then must apply for CNIC)

FEES:
- Standard: Rs. 50 (5-10 working days)
- Executive: Rs. 500

Documents required:
- Union Council birth certificate
- CNIC of both parents

How to apply:
- Children under 1 year: Apply via Pak ID App or online at id.nadra.gov.pk
- Children over 1 year: Visit NADRA Registration Center in person
- Child must be present
- Fingerprints required for children aged 10 and above

========================================
FAMILY REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE (FRC)
========================================
Documents all family members linked in NADRA database. Required for legal matters, property, inheritance, visa applications.

How to apply:
- Apply online at id.nadra.gov.pk
- Receive FRC as PDF via email within 1-2 working days

========================================
PASSPORT
========================================
Issued by Directorate General of Immigration and Passports (DGIP), not NADRA. However CNIC/NICOP is required to apply.

Online portal: onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk
Fee payment: Passport Fee Asaan App or e-Payment Web Portal

Types:
- Ordinary Machine Readable Passport (MRP)
- e-Passport (available from all field offices since August 2023)

Documents required:
- Original valid CNIC/NICOP with photocopy
- Previous passport with photocopy (if issued)
- Bank fee receipt or e-payment confirmation
- For government employees: NOC from department
- For minors aged 10+: Updated B-Form with fingerprints and photo

Important notes:
- You can apply from any passport office regardless of your address on CNIC
- Online passport renewal is available for both inland and overseas Pakistanis
- Lost passport: Double fee charged for first loss, quadruple for second loss
- Must file police report for lost passport

PASSPORT OFFICES:
- Available in all major cities across Pakistan
- Some offices open 24/7 including weekends
- Check dgip.gov.pk for nearest office

========================================
POC (PAKISTAN ORIGIN CARD)
========================================
For foreign nationals with Pakistani roots or foreign spouses of Pakistani citizens.

Eligibility:
- You were a Pakistani national but surrendered nationality
- Foreign national whose parents or grandparents were Pakistani
- Foreign national with close Pakistani relative (brother, sister, uncle, aunt)
- Foreign national married to a Pakistani citizen

Validity:
- Spouse-based POC: 5 years
- Ex-Pakistani POC: 7 years

========================================
HOW TO CHECK YOUR CNIC STATUS ONLINE
========================================
1. Visit NADRA's official website: nadra.gov.pk
2. Go to CNIC Verification Portal
3. Enter your 13-digit CNIC number
4. View your registration status and details

Track application by SMS:
- Send your tracking ID to 8400
- Receive status updates via SMS

========================================
PAYMENT METHODS
========================================
At NADRA Centers: Cash accepted
Online applications: Credit/debit card or e-payment franchise (Sahulat)
Passport fee: Via Passport Fee Asaan App or Web Portal

========================================
IMPORTANT NOTES
========================================
- New CNIC is FREE under normal processing
- Keep your receipt until you receive your card
- Executive service available at Mega Centers and specific NRCs
- If any information is wrong on your card, ask the issuing office to correct it immediately
- To modify name, father name, or date of birth on passport: First modify CNIC/NICOP, then apply for new passport
