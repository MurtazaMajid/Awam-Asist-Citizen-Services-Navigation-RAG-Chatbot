PROPERTY & LAND RECORDS - COMPLETE CITIZEN GUIDE
=================================================
Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA) | Arazi Record Centers
Applicable: Punjab Residents | Updated: 2025

SECTION 1: KEY TERMS EXPLAINED (SIMPLE URDU/ENGLISH)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FARD (فرد):         Official copy of land ownership record
                    Proof that you own the land — like a title deed

MUTATION (انتقال):  Updating land records when ownership changes
                    Required after: purchase, inheritance, gift

JAMABANDI (جمابندی): Complete record of all land in a village/area
                    Contains: owner names, area, land type

KHASRA (خسرہ):     Unique plot number assigned to every piece of land

KHAT:              Owner's complete land record

TATIMMA:           Correction in existing land record

REGISTRY:          Sale deed / purchase agreement registered at
                   Sub-Registrar office

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 2: HOW TO GET FARD (فرد حاصل کریں)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Fard is the most commonly needed document — proof of land ownership.

METHOD 1 — Online (Easiest):
1. Visit: punjab-zameen.gov.pk OR plra.punjab.gov.pk
2. Click "Get Fard Online"
3. Select District → Tehsil → Village/Mouza
4. Enter Khasra number or owner CNIC
5. View and download/print Fard
6. Pay Rs. 300–500 fee online (via Easypaisa, JazzCash, bank)

METHOD 2 — Arazi Record Center (ARC):
1. Visit nearest Arazi Record Center (ARC) in your tehsil
2. Fill Fard request form
3. Submit with CNIC copy
4. Pay fee (Rs. 300–500)
5. Fard printed same day or within 1–2 days

DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
- CNIC (original)
- Khasra number (if known) or owner name
- District, Tehsil, Village/Mouza name
- Fee payment

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 3: MUTATION (INTIQAL) — OWNERSHIP TRANSFER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Mutation is done when land ownership changes hands.
MUST be done after: buying land, inheriting land, receiving as gift.

TYPES OF MUTATION:
a) Sale Mutation    — After buying land (Bai)
b) Inheritance      — After death of owner (Wirassat)
c) Gift (Hiba)      — Land gifted to family member
d) Exchange         — Two parties swap land
e) Court Order      — After court decree

DOCUMENTS FOR SALE MUTATION:
- Original registered sale deed (Registry)
- CNIC of buyer and seller
- Fard of land (recent)
- Affidavit (if required)
- Mutation fee payment

DOCUMENTS FOR INHERITANCE MUTATION:
- Death certificate of deceased owner
- Legal heir certificate (from NADRA or court)
- CNIC of all legal heirs
- Family registration certificate (FRC)
- Recent Fard

STEP-BY-STEP MUTATION PROCESS:
1. Visit Arazi Record Center (ARC) in land's tehsil
2. Submit application with all documents
3. Patwari verifies documents on-ground
4. Notice issued (15 days for objections)
5. If no objection — mutation approved and recorded
6. New Fard issued with updated ownership

TIME: Usually 30–60 days (can be longer in contested cases)

SECTION 4: PROPERTY REGISTRATION (REGISTRY)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Registry = Formal registration of sale deed at Sub-Registrar office.
THIS MUST BE DONE BEFORE MUTATION.

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. Both buyer and seller visit Sub-Registrar office together
2. Bring: Sale agreement, CNICs, 2 witnesses with CNICs, Fard
3. Sub-Registrar verifies documents
4. Stamp duty and registration fee paid
5. Sale deed signed and thumb-printed by both parties
6. Registered copy given — keep this SAFE forever

STAMP DUTY:
- Varies by property value and district
- Generally 3–5% of property value
- Check current rates at district registration office

SECTION 5: CHECKING LAND ONLINE (Verification)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

BEFORE BUYING ANY PROPERTY — always verify:

1. Visit: punjab-zameen.gov.pk
2. Search by owner CNIC or Khasra number
3. Check:
   - Current owner name (matches seller?)
   - Any encumbrance / loans on land?
   - Land area matches what seller claims?
   - Land type (agricultural / residential / commercial)?

WARNING: If CNIC in records does NOT match seller — do not buy.
         Always verify before paying any money.

SECTION 6: CORRECTIONS IN LAND RECORDS (TATIMMA)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

If there is an error in your land records (wrong name, area, etc.):

1. Apply at ARC with: CNIC + proof of correct information + Fard
2. Patwari reviews and recommends correction
3. Approved by Revenue Officer
4. Records updated

For major disputes: File case in Revenue Court (tehsil level)

SECTION 7: COMMON LAND FRAUDS — BE CAREFUL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚠ Seller shows old Fard — always ask for Fard dated within 30 days
⚠ Seller not actual owner — always verify CNIC in official records
⚠ Land sold to multiple people — check for any encumbrances
⚠ Agricultural land sold as residential — verify land type
⚠ Fake mutations — always verify at ARC before payment
