PAKISTAN CITIZENS PORTAL (PCP) - COMPLETE GUIDE
================================================
Prime Minister's Performance Delivery Unit (PMDU)
File Complaints Against ANY Government Department | Updated: 2025

SECTION 1: WHAT IS PAKISTAN CITIZENS PORTAL?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Pakistan Citizens Portal (PCP) is the official government app where
you can file complaints against ANY government department or official.

USE IT WHEN:
- A government office is not helping you
- An officer asked for bribe (corruption)
- Your application is stuck for too long
- You received wrong/unfair treatment from government
- You want to report a public issue (bad road, broken pipe, etc.)
- You need guidance on any government service

IMPORTANT: PCP does not resolve issues itself — it forwards your
complaint to the relevant department and tracks if they respond.

SECTION 2: HOW TO INSTALL & REGISTER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

INSTALL:
- Android: Search "Pakistan Citizens Portal" on Google Play Store
- iPhone: Search "Pakistan Citizens Portal" on App Store
- Web: web.citizenportal.gov.pk

REGISTRATION (One time only):
1. Open the app
2. Click "Register"
3. Enter your CNIC number
4. Enter your mobile number (must be on CNIC)
5. OTP sent to your mobile — enter it
6. Set password
7. Registration complete

NOTE: You can also register via web browser if you don't have
      a smartphone.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 3: HOW TO FILE A COMPLAINT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

STEP-BY-STEP:
1. Login to PCP app
2. Tap the "+" (plus) button to start new complaint
3. Select complaint TYPE:
   - Personal complaint (your own issue)
   - Social complaint (public/community issue)
   - Suggestion
4. Select concerned DEPARTMENT from the list
   (Example: NADRA, WASA, Police, Education Dept.)
5. Select your PROVINCE and DISTRICT
6. Write your complaint in the text box
   - Be clear and specific
   - Mention dates, officer names if known
   - Include your CNIC / reference numbers
7. Attach photos/documents if available
8. Submit complaint

COMPLAINT ID: After submission you get a unique Complaint ID.
SAVE THIS — you need it to track your complaint.

SECTION 4: HOW TO WRITE AN EFFECTIVE COMPLAINT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

BAD COMPLAINT (too vague):
"NADRA is not helping me. Please resolve my issue."

GOOD COMPLAINT (specific and clear):
"I visited NADRA office Rawalpindi Saddar on 15 April 2025 for
CNIC renewal. Officer Mr. [Name] at Window 3 refused to process
my application without reason and asked me to come back next week.
My token number was A-245. My CNIC is 37405-XXXXXXX-X.
This is my 3rd visit and the issue is still unresolved."

TIPS:
✔ Mention date(s) of your visit/incident
✔ Name the specific office and location
✔ Include officer name or window number if known
✔ Attach photo of any official receipt or document
✔ State exactly what you want resolved
✔ Keep it factual — no abusive language

SECTION 5: TRACKING YOUR COMPLAINT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

IN THE APP:
- Go to "My Complaints"
- Select your complaint to see status

STATUSES:
- Pending      → Forwarded to department, awaiting their response
- In Progress  → Department has accepted and is working on it
- Resolved     → Department has marked it resolved
- Dropped      → Complaint was not valid / not in their jurisdiction

TIME LIMITS:
- Departments are given a deadline to respond (usually 15–30 days)
- If not resolved in time → escalates to higher authority automatically

IF MARKED RESOLVED BUT NOT ACTUALLY RESOLVED:
1. Rate the resolution as "Not Satisfied"
2. Complaint re-opens and escalates
3. DO NOT just close the app — your feedback matters

SECTION 6: WHAT COMPLAINTS DOES PCP HANDLE?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PCP handles complaints for ALL government departments including:

Federal:
✔ NADRA (CNIC delays, errors)
✔ FBR (tax issues)
✔ Passport Office (delays)
✔ PIA (flight issues)
✔ Pakistan Railways
✔ HEC (scholarship issues)
✔ Ministry of Interior

Provincial (Punjab, KP, Sindh, Balochistan):
✔ Police (FIR, misbehaviour)
✔ Education Department (school issues)
✔ Health Department (hospital complaints)
✔ WASA (water issues)
✔ SNGPL (gas issues)
✔ Revenue Department (land record issues)
✔ Local Government (roads, sewerage)

SECTION 7: REPORTING CORRUPTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

If an officer asked for BRIBE (rishwat):

Option 1 — PCP App:
- File complaint → Select "Corruption / Bribery"
- Name the officer, office, amount demanded, date

Option 2 — NAB (National Accountability Bureau):
- Online complaint: nab.gov.pk
- Helpline: 1800-NAB-NAB (1800-622-622)

Option 3 — ACE Punjab (Anti-Corruption Establishment):
- Helpline: 0800-02345 (toll free)
- Website: ace.punjab.gov.pk
