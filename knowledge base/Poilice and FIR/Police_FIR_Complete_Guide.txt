POLICE COMPLAINTS AND FIR - CITIZEN GUIDE
Pakistan Law Enforcement Services

===========================================
WHAT IS AN FIR?
===========================================

An FIR (First Information Report) is the official written document prepared by police when a crime is reported. It is governed by Section 154 of the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC) 1898.

Filing an FIR is your legal right. It officially starts the criminal investigation process. Without an FIR, police cannot formally investigate most crimes.

===========================================
TYPES OF OFFENCES
===========================================

Cognizable Offence:
A serious crime where police can arrest without a warrant and investigate without court permission. FIR is required.
Examples: Murder, robbery, theft, kidnapping, assault, rape, house breaking

Non-Cognizable Offence:
A less serious crime where police need a court order before arresting. A complaint is filed instead of FIR.
Examples: Minor disputes, minor assault without injury, defamation

===========================================
WHO CAN FILE AN FIR?
===========================================

Anyone can file an FIR, not just the victim. This includes:
- The victim of the crime
- A witness who saw the crime
- A family member of the victim
- Any concerned citizen who has knowledge of the offence

You do not need a lawyer to file an FIR.

===========================================
HOW TO FILE AN FIR (STEP BY STEP)
===========================================

Step 1: Go to the police station
Visit the police station in whose jurisdiction the crime occurred. If you are unsure which station, go to the nearest one.

Step 2: Meet the Duty Officer
Ask to speak with the Station House Officer (SHO) or the Duty Officer at the front desk.

Step 3: Give your statement
Describe the crime clearly. Include:
- What happened (nature of the offence)
- Where it happened (exact location)
- When it happened (date and time)
- Who did it (suspect details if known)
- Any evidence or witnesses

Step 4: Get the FIR read back to you
After police write the FIR, ask them to read it back to you. This is your legal right. Make sure all details are correct.

Step 5: Sign the FIR
Once you are satisfied, sign the FIR. If you cannot read or write, use your left thumb impression.

Step 6: Get your free copy
Ask for a copy of the FIR immediately. Police must provide it free of cost. Keep this copy safe as it is your proof that the complaint was registered.

===========================================
WHAT IF POLICE REFUSE TO REGISTER THE FIR?
===========================================

Police refusal to register an FIR for a cognizable offence is illegal.

If police refuse, you have these legal options:

Option 1 - Complain to senior police:
Submit a written complaint to the District Police Officer (DPO) or Superintendent of Police (SP) of your area.

Option 2 - Contact Police Helpline:
Punjab: Call 1787 or visit any police Front Desk
National: Pakistan Citizen Portal (complaints.pakistan.gov.pk)

Option 3 - Go to Court:
File an application before the Sessions Court (Magistrate) under Section 22-A & 22-B of the CrPC. The court can legally direct the police to register your FIR. This is a powerful legal remedy that is very effective.

Option 4 - High Court:
File a petition in the High Court under Article 199 of the Constitution of Pakistan.

===========================================
YOUR RIGHTS DURING POLICE INVESTIGATION
===========================================

After filing FIR:
- You have the right to know the progress of your case
- You can request status updates from the investigating officer
- You can approach the court if police are not investigating properly

If you are arrested:
- You must be informed of the reason for arrest
- You have the right to inform a family member or lawyer
- You cannot be detained for more than 24 hours without being presented before a court
- You have the right to bail in bailable offences

===========================================
BAIL
===========================================

Bailable offence:
Police must grant bail as a right. You can also approach a Magistrate.

Non-bailable offence:
Only a court can grant bail. You need to apply before a Sessions Court or High Court. A lawyer is strongly recommended for non-bailable bail applications.

===========================================
COMMON SITUATIONS AND WHAT TO DO
===========================================

Theft or robbery:
File FIR at nearest police station immediately. Bring any evidence (CCTV footage if available, witness names, description of stolen items).

Phone snatching:
File FIR at nearest police station. Also report to PTA (Pakistan Telecommunication Authority) to block the stolen phone. IMEI number of your phone is useful - usually found on the original box.

Domestic violence:
Call 1099 (Women's helpline in Punjab) or go to the nearest police station. You can also contact Rescue 1122 or the Women's Protection Authority.

Cybercrime (online fraud, harassment):
Report to FIA Cybercrime Wing:
- Website: fia.gov.pk/en/cybercrime.html
- Call: 9911 (FIA Cybercrime helpline)

Vehicle theft:
File FIR immediately and also report to MTMIS (Motor Transport Management Information System) to block the vehicle registration.

Missing person:
Report to the nearest police station and file a missing person report. Also check with hospitals and contact Edhi Foundation: 115.

===========================================
IMPORTANT WARNINGS
===========================================

Filing a false FIR is a crime under Sections 182 and 211 of the Pakistan Penal Code (PPC). It can result in imprisonment and fine.

Always file FIR based on facts. Do not add false information even if you are angry about the crime.

File FIR as soon as possible. Delay weakens evidence and reduces chances of justice.

Keep your FIR copy safe. It is your proof and you will need it for follow-up.
