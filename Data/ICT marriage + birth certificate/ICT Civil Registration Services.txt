=============================================================
PART 1: MARRIAGE REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE
=============================================================

WHAT IS A MARRIAGE REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE?
----------------------------------------------
A Marriage Registration Certificate (MRC) is a legal document that serves as official proof of marriage in Pakistan. It is mandatory by law to register every marriage. The certificate includes names of bride and groom, their ages, and date and place of marriage.

It is commonly called "NADRA Marriage Certificate" but NADRA does not directly issue it. It is issued by Union Council, TMA, Cantonment Board, or Arbitration Council using NADRA's system.

WHY IS IT IMPORTANT?
---------------------
- Required for visa applications abroad (UK, US, and other countries)
- Needed for opening joint bank accounts
- Required for children's passport and visa applications
- Proof of marriage for all legal and administrative purposes
- Internationally recognized document

WHERE TO APPLY - ISLAMABAD
---------------------------
Citizen Facilitation Center
G-11/4, Islamabad
Timings: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Friday break: 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM
Processing time: 7 days
Fee: Rs. 200

Important note for Islamabad:
- Marriage must be registered by a registrar of the Sectorial area of Islamabad
- If marriage was registered in rural area of Islamabad or any other region, apply to concerned Union Council

WHERE TO APPLY - OTHER CITIES
-------------------------------
- Lahore, Karachi, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Hyderabad, Peshawar: Visit your local Union Council
- Cantonment areas: Visit Cantonment Board office
- Small cities: Visit TMA (Tehsil Municipal Administration) office

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
-------------------
- Original Nikkah Nama (marriage contract)
- Attested copy of Nikkah Nama
- Copy of CNIC of bride and groom
- Copy of CNIC of bride's father and groom's father
- Copy of passport (if either party is a foreign national)
- Copy of B-Form (if either party is under 18)
- Copy of CNIC of the applicant

Important notes:
- All documents must be in a file cover
- Applicant must appear in person to submit documents
- Bride must be at least 18 years of age at time of marriage (by law)
- Two adult male witnesses required for valid Nikah

STEP BY STEP PROCESS
---------------------
1. Record checking
2. Data entry
3. Proof reading
4. Check proof reading
5. Final print
6. Signature by Chairman Arbitration Council
7. Collect certificate after processing time

PROCESSING TIME AND FEE
------------------------
- Islamabad (CFC): 7 days, Rs. 200
- NADRA Marriage Certificate (general): Rs. 5,000, 3-5 working days
- Urgent processing: 1-2 days at some offices (additional fee)

SECOND MARRIAGE
----------------
- Must provide original Divorce Certificate (Talaq Nama) from previous marriage
- Or Death Certificate if previous spouse is deceased

FOREIGN SPOUSE
---------------
- Additional documents required including passport and NOC from foreign embassy
- At least one party must have Pakistani CNIC or NICOP

MARRIAGE CERTIFICATE ATTESTATION (FOR USE ABROAD)
---------------------------------------------------
If you need your marriage certificate for use in another country:
1. Get MRC from Union Council/CFC
2. Get it attested by Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) Pakistan
   - MOFA offices in Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta
3. Submit to destination country's embassy in Pakistan

ERRORS ON CERTIFICATE
----------------------
- Check all details carefully when you receive the certificate
- Match names, spellings, dates, ages with your official documents
- If there is an error, ask the issuing office to correct it immediately

PART 2: BIRTH CERTIFICATE
=============================================================

WHAT IS A BIRTH CERTIFICATE?
------------------------------
A birth certificate is an official document issued to record a person's birth. It is proof of date and place of birth. In Pakistan, NADRA has developed a computerized birth certificate system but does not directly issue certificates — local Union Councils and other bodies issue them using NADRA's system.

WHERE TO APPLY
---------------
- Major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Hyderabad, Peshawar): Visit your local Union Council
- Cantonment areas: Visit Cantonment Board office
- Small cities: TMA (Tehsil Municipal Administration) office
- Islamabad: CDA office or Citizen Facilitation Center, G-11/4

For Islamabad:
Location: Citizen Facilitation Center, Mauve Area, G-11/4, Islamabad
Timings: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR BIRTH CERTIFICATE
------------------------------------------
- Hospital birth record or certificate from traditional birth attendant
- Copy of parents' CNIC
- Completed application form (available at issuing office)

Important: Register your child's birth within 60 days at the concerned Union Council.

STEP BY STEP PROCESS
---------------------
1. Gather required documents
2. Visit your local Union Council or relevant issuing office
3. Fill out application form
4. Submit application with documents
5. Receive receipt with collection date
6. Collect birth certificate after processing

PART 3: NADRA B-FORM (CHILD REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE - CRC)
=============================================================

WHAT IS A B-FORM?
------------------
The B-Form (also called Child Registration Certificate or CRC) is the first official identity document issued to a child in Pakistan by NADRA. It links the child with parents in the national database and assigns a unique 13-digit CNIC number that stays with the child for life. At age 18, this becomes their CNIC.

WHY IS IT NEEDED?
------------------
- Required for school admission
- Required for passport application
- Needed for medical treatment
- Required for all legal and government services

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
-------------------
- Union Council birth certificate of the child
- CNIC of both parents
- Parents must have valid CNIC or NICOP

FEES
-----
- Standard: Rs. 50
- Executive (urgent): Rs. 500

HOW TO APPLY
-------------
For children under 1 year:
- Apply online through Pak ID app or visit id.nadra.gov.pk
- OR visit nearest NADRA Registration Center (NRC)

For children over 1 year:
- Must visit nearest NADRA Registration Center in person
- Child must be present
- Both parents ideally present (one applicant, one verifier)
- If only one parent available, application must be verified by a Gazetted Officer or public representative (MNA, MPA, or municipal official)
- Fingerprints required for children aged 10 and above

PROCESSING TIME
---------------
- 5-10 working days

CHECK APPLICATION STATUS
-------------------------
- Through Pak ID app
- Send tracking ID via SMS to 8400
- Visit nearest NADRA center

=============================================================
PART 4: DEATH CERTIFICATE
=============================================================
Death certificate is issued by Union Council of the area where the person lived.

Required documents:
- Information about deceased (name, date and place of death)
- CNIC of deceased
- CNIC of applicant (family member)

Visit your nearest Union Council to apply.
