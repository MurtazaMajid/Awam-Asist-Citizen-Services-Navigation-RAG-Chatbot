IESCO Electricity Services - Citizen Guide
==========================================

NEW ELECTRICITY CONNECTION
---------------------------
How to apply:
- Visit your nearest IESCO sub-division office in person
- You can also apply by telephone, mail, or online via IESCO website
- A one-window operation facility is available at each sub-division

Documents required for new connection:
- Ownership proof of premises (property documents)
- Attested copy of CNIC of applicant
- Approved map or site plan (can be relaxed in rural areas)
- Wiring test report from a Pakistan Engineering Council registered electrical engineer

Process after application:
1. Submit application with documents
2. IESCO conducts site survey
3. Demand notice issued for connection charges and security deposit
4. Pay charges at designated bank branch
5. Connection installed within stipulated time

Connection timeline:
- Small connections (up to 15 kW): approximately 15-20 days
- Medium connections (15-70 kW): approximately 25-30 days
- Large connections (above 70 kW): approximately 30-45 days

CONNECTION CHARGES (LUMP SUM) FOR RESIDENTIAL & COMMERCIAL (Tariff A1 and A2)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Single Phase Meter:
- Service line up to 20 meters: Rs. 8,500
- Service line 20 to 30 meters: Rs. 10,500
- Service line 30 to 40 meters: Rs. 11,500
- Service line 40 to 100 meters: Rs. 11,000 + Rs. 800 per meter above 40 meters
- Service line 100 to 160 meters: Rs. 60,000 + Rs. 1,000 per meter above 100 meters

Three Phase Meter:
- Service line up to 20 meters: Rs. 36,500
- Service line up to 30 meters: Rs. 43,000
- Service line up to 40 meters: Rs. 50,000
- Service line above 40 meters: Actual cost as per IESCO store rates

SECURITY DEPOSIT RATES
----------------------
Residential (A-1):
- Urban: Rs. 1,220 per kW
- Rural: Rs. 610 per kW

Commercial (A-2):
- Urban: Rs. 1,810 per kW
- Rural: Rs. 920 per kW

Industrial:
- B1: Rs. 1,580 per kW
- B2: Rs. 2,010 per kW
- B3: Rs. 2,890 per kW
- B4: Rs. 3,560 per kW

ELECTRICITY BILLING
-------------------
- Meter reading is taken every month by IESCO staff
- Bill is delivered to your premises within one day of generation
- Bill is also available on IESCO website for download and payment
- Payment can be made at any commercial bank branch or post office

What your bill contains:
- Consumer name and reference number
- Billing period and units consumed
- Total cost of electricity as per applicable tariff
- GST (General Sales Tax)
- Amount payable within due date
- Billing history for last 12 months

DISCONNECTION
-------------
Your connection can be disconnected if:
- You have not paid your electricity bill
- You are involved in electricity theft
- You illegally reconnected after disconnection
- Your load exceeds sanctioned load
- You request disconnection yourself

Reconnection:
- Pay all outstanding dues to get reconnected
- If disconnected for more than 365 days, IESCO may remove the meter
- If disconnected for more than 3 years, you must apply for a new connection

CONSUMER COMPLAINTS
-------------------
Types of complaints you can file:
- New connection issues
- Wrong meter reading or billing errors
- Electricity supply failures
- Low voltage or fluctuations
- Detection bills disputes

How to file a complaint:
- Visit nearest IESCO Consumer Services Center
- Use IESCO online complaint management system
- Contact One Window Operations facility at your sub-division

Complaint resolution timeframes:
- Billing complaints: resolved within 15 days
- Technical complaints (low voltage, fluctuations): attended within 24-48 hours
- Electricity leakage complaints: attended immediately

CHANGE OF NAME
--------------
Documents required:
- Ownership proof / sale deed
- Copy of last paid bill (no arrears should be pending)
- Attested copy of CNIC of applicant
- Power Supply Contract

THEFT OF ELECTRICITY
--------------------
- Electricity theft is a criminal offense
- IESCO can file an FIR against anyone found stealing electricity
- Detection bills can be issued for up to 6 months of estimated theft

ELIGIBILITY FOR NEW CONNECTION
------------------------------
You are eligible for a new electricity connection if:
- You submit a proper application with required documents
- Your wiring is tested and certified by a PEC registered electrical engineer
- You pay the required security deposit
- No electricity bill arrears are outstanding at the premises
- You have not been convicted of electricity theft
