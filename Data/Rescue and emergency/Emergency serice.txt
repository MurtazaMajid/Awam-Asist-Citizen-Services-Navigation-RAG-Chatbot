========================================================
RESCUE 1122
========================================================
Pakistan's leading government emergency service. Covers fire, rescue, and medical emergencies.

Helpline: 1122
Available: 24/7
Response time: Average 7 minutes
Coverage: All 36 districts of Punjab, KPK, Azad Jammu & Kashmir, Gilgit-Baltistan
Also available in: Karachi (Sindh Emergency Rescue Service)

In Islamabad:
CARES 1122 (Capital Ambulance, Rescue and Emergency Services)
Operated by CDA and MCI

Services provided:
- Emergency ambulance
- Road traffic accident response
- Building collapse rescue
- Fire fighting and fire prevention
- Hazardous material incidents
- Flood and disaster rescue
- Water rescue
- High-angle and confined space rescue
- Air ambulance (for critical patient transfers from DHQ hospitals to major cities)
- Community Safety Program and training

History:
- Established in 2004 under Punjab Emergency Service Act 2006
- First modern integrated emergency service in Pakistan
- Over 97% rescue operation success rate

When to call 1122:
- Road accidents
- House or building fires
- Medical emergencies
- Drowning
- Building collapse
- Any life threatening situation

========================================================
PAKISTAN EMERGENCY HELPLINE (PEHL) - 911
========================================================
Helpline: 911
Available: Nationwide on landline and mobile

Single number that connects you to:
- Police
- Fire Brigade
- Ambulance service
- National Highways and Motorway Police (NH&MP)

========================================================
EDHI FOUNDATION AMBULANCE
========================================================
Helpline: 115
Available: 24/7, nationwide
Service: FREE of charge

One of the largest ambulance networks in the world with over 1,800 vehicles across Pakistan. Also has air ambulance service.

Coverage: All major cities across Pakistan
Headquarters: Karachi

========================================================
CHHIPA AMBULANCE SERVICE
========================================================
Helpline: 1021 (also listed as 1020)
Available: 24/7
Response time: Within 5 minutes

One of the largest ambulance fleets in Pakistan. Ambulances equipped with basic medical facilities and oxygen cylinders. Emergency centers located near all major hospitals.

Strong presence in: Sindh (especially Karachi) and other major cities

========================================================
WHAT TO DO IN DIFFERENT EMERGENCIES
========================================================

ROAD ACCIDENT:
1. Call 1122 immediately
2. Do not move injured persons unless there is immediate danger (fire, flooding)
3. Keep the scene clear for rescue teams
4. Note the location precisely to tell rescue teams

FIRE:
1. Call 16 or 1122
2. Evacuate everyone from the building immediately
3. Do not use elevators
4. Close doors behind you to slow fire spread
5. Meet at a safe distance from the building

MEDICAL EMERGENCY:
1. Call 1122 or 115 (Edhi) or 1021 (Chhipa)
2. Stay calm and describe the emergency clearly
3. Give exact address and landmarks
4. Stay on the line with the operator
5. Do not move the patient unless absolutely necessary

CRIME/THEFT/ROBBERY:
1. Call 15 (Police Madadgar)
2. Note description of suspects if possible
3. Do not confront suspects
4. Preserve the crime scene

MISSING PERSON:
1. Call 15 (Police Madadgar)
2. Provide recent photo and description
3. Also contact NADRA helpline: 051-111-786-100

CHILD ABUSE OR WOMEN IN DANGER:
- Shaheen helpline (for women): 1043
- Child Protection: Contact local police via 15

Contact your nearest Rescue 1122 station for training information.
